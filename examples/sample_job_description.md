# Job Title: Data Analyst – Marketing Analytics (FinTech)

> Fictional demo job description for Example FinTech Corp.

**About the job**
Example FinTech Corp is looking for a data-driven Data Analyst to join its growth team. Previous experience in financial services or FinTech is essential.

The ideal candidate has a strong understanding of data and can use analytics to optimize marketing campaigns and strategies. You will collect, analyze and interpret data to inform decisions and drive results.

Experience in trading or brokerage products is a plus.

**Key Responsibilities**
- Develop and implement data-driven strategies to measure and analyze campaigns.
- Collect and analyze data from various sources (social media, media monitoring, website analytics, etc.).
- Identify key performance indicators (KPIs) and develop reporting dashboards.
- Provide actionable insights to the team to inform decision-making.
- Use data analytics tools to uncover trends and patterns in audience behavior.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate data insights into overall business strategy.

**The Ideal Candidate**
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to translate data into actionable insights.
- Experience with data analytics tools and platforms (SQL, Python, BI tools).
- Understanding of digital marketing and social media analytics.
- Experience with A/B testing and campaign attribution is a plus.
